# Troubleshooting

## An import asks me to paste text

Some pages cannot be read automatically: Instagram showing a login page, a site built with JavaScript, a YouTube video with no captions. Copy the text yourself and paste it into the box. See [Importing a recipe](03-importing-a-recipe.md).

## "That doesn't look like a recipe"

Nabbit checked the text and thinks it is something else. The reason is shown. If it is a recipe after all, choose **Import anyway**.

## The amounts look wrong

Check the **AI-assisted** or **check quantities** banners. Use **Edit** to fix amounts before saving. The preview shows the amounts as written in the source, so compare against the original.

## The photo is missing

A photo is only saved if one was found and chosen. In the preview tap **Show photo options** to pick one, or upload your own. If a photo fails to save, Nabbit saves the recipe without it and tells you.

## I hit an import limit

There is a limit of 30 imports per hour and a daily limit on AI-assisted reads. Wait and try again later.

## "The recipe or list has changed"

Another device of yours (or another tab) saved a change first. Choose **Reload**, then redo your change.

## A deleted recipe

Deleting a recipe offers **Undo** straight away. Deleted recipes are kept for 30 days before they are removed for good.

## Nabbit shows an old version

Installed apps tell you when a new version is ready: choose **Reload** on the message. If you do not see it, close and reopen the app.

## Signing in or out

If you are sent back to the sign-in page unexpectedly, your session expired. Sign in again and Nabbit returns you to where you were, including a shared link waiting to be imported. **Sign out** is in the avatar menu.

## Nothing loads

Nabbit needs an internet connection and does not keep your recipes or lists offline.
