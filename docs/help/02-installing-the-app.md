# Installing the app

Nabbit is a progressive web app (PWA). Installing it gives you an icon on your home screen or desktop, a full-screen window with no browser bars, and on Android a way to send links to Nabbit straight from other apps.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open Nabbit in Chrome and sign in.
2. Open the Chrome menu and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Confirm. The Nabbit icon appears with your other apps.

Once installed, Nabbit also appears in Android's share sheet. See [Importing a recipe](03-importing-a-recipe.md).

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open Nabbit in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

The share-sheet shortcut is an Android feature. On iPhone, copy the link and paste it into the import screen.

## Desktop (Chrome or Edge)

Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose **Install Nabbit**.

## Updates

When a new version is released, an installed Nabbit shows **A new version is available**. Tap **Reload** to update. It never reloads on its own, so you will not lose an edit in progress.

## Offline

Installing does not make Nabbit work offline. The app itself loads from the device, but recipes and lists are always fetched from the server.
